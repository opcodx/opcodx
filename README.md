## <img src="https://opcodx.com/brand/opcodx-emblem-dark.svg" width="24" alt=""> About OPCODX

**OPCODX** is an independent technology brand focused on cybersecurity, secure software engineering, infrastructure technologies, and intelligent automation.

Our mission is to explore and develop practical digital solutions that prioritize security, reliability, privacy, and long-term maintainability.

We bring together security-oriented thinking and modern engineering practices to explore new ideas, build technical capabilities, and continuously improve software and infrastructure solutions.

**Our guiding principles:**

- **Security by Design** — Integrating security throughout the engineering lifecycle.
- **Privacy by Default** — Respecting data ownership, confidentiality, and digital sovereignty.
- **Engineering Excellence** — Prioritizing maintainability, reliability, and quality.
- **Continuous Innovation** — Learning, experimenting, and improving through technology.
